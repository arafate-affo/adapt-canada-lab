Business adaptation to the 2025-2026 tariff shock: Canada (prototype dashboard)

Built from Statistics Canada (CSBC, trade), Elections Canada, open.canada.ca and the US Census BTOS. Prototype index, see methodology in the repository docs.

1. Adaptation index by province

2. Index over the tariff episode (click a province in the legend)

Shaded quarters measure actions taken; the others, actions planned over the next 12 months.

3. Planned responses by industry, Canada, latest quarter

Stacked shares of businesses planning each action (next 12 months). Hover for the share reporting a negative tariff impact.

4. Business AI adoption by sector: US vs Canada

US: BTOS 2026 biweekly average, AI used in the last two weeks. Canada: CSBC Q2 2026, AI used in the last 12 months. Definitions differ; indicative.

5. Exports to the United States by province

Domestic exports, customs basis, January-September of each year (StatCan 12-10-0175-01). Hover for the US share of exports in 2024.

6. Federal tariff-response awards disclosed by province (lower bound)

Agreements mentioning tariffs in the proactive disclosure of grants and contributions (mostly the Regional Tariff Response Initiative); several agencies have not yet disclosed 2026 awards.
